## Key question 1: profile of each direct rival (checks, install, licence, traction, pricing, integrations, launch)

### Takeaway
Six 2026 rivals (squirrelscan, GeoReady, Crawlie, geo-score, geolint, SEOmator) now ship the same surface RankCLI has (free CLI, Action, MCP, skills), and the installed base is led by squirrelscan on npm (3,784/wk) and GeoReady on PyPI (4,201/wk), both 20 to 26 times RankCLI's CLI downloads; the incumbents (Lighthouse CI 2.46M/wk, Unlighthouse 108K/wk, sitespeed.io 5K/wk) are performance tools whose SEO coverage is Lighthouse's 11 audits. A long tail of at least 25 npm/PyPI packages created in 2026 use the words "GEO", "AI search readiness" or "linter" in their one-liners, most under 300 downloads a week.
